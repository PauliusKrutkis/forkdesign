Design notes

The whole popup is one conversation: your note, the agent's variants, and replies share a single transcript with an always-visible composer.

Agent is the default verb

The Fix ⇄ Comment pill resolves your "reply vs. instruction" worry without guessing. Fix is lit by default — ⌘↵ always does the lit action. Try toggling it.

A turn = a variant group

One instruction → 2–4 variant cards, one marked live on page. "Use", delete, and live-state live on the cards — so the old bottom action bar is gone.

⌘↵ to send, Enter = newline

Your content is considered, multi-line design instruction — not quick chat. So we keep your codebase's existing ⌘↵ convention rather than ChatGPT's bare Enter.

Type does the talking

Human voice is set in a warm grotesque; every machine surface — the agent, ids, timestamps — is mono. You can feel who's speaking.
